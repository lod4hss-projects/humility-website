Source Text

humilitas sic descripta imitanda, primo quia sue angustie est paciens tolleratrix; ut matrona a viro suo iniuriose tractata pacienter sustinet ne virum dignum vituperio reddat, and if perchance, from the injury of a beating, a mark appears outwardly for the reproach, she carefully conceals it, asserting that she incurred such harm from elsewhere. Ecclesiastici 2 dicitur: "Aurum et argentum in igne probantur," etc. Unde Augustinus: "Beati, inquit, humiles qui, etsi quando fieri solet ut iniustas audiant increpaciones, amplius se Christo humilient et minus se audire quam meruerunt proclamant."

Critical Notes

Augustinian attribution UNVERIFIED. 'Beati humiles' is not a Vulgate beatitude (cf. Matt 5:5 'beati mites'). Check CCL indices, the Augustinus-Lexikon, and Clavis Fontium for the sentence; treat as possibly florilegetic or pseudo-Augustinian until confirmed. Do not cite as Augustine.
  • 'etc.' in c4 must be resolved as scribal vs editorial.
  • The definition promised by 'sic descripta' is absent. If the corpus is to support definitional claims, this passage should be re-extracted at argument boundaries to include the antecedent description and the secundo.
